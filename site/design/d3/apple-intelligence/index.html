Apple Intelligence

On this page

  • What it includes
  • Which devices
  • Languages and regions
  • Storage, limits and cost

Apple describes the next generation of Apple Intelligence as integrated deeply into the operating system, and "private at every step".[1][3] It is automatically available on eligible devices when you update to the 27.0 software releases.[3]

What it includes

Apple's page names these among its features: Siri AI, Visual Intelligence, photo editing tools, Image Playground, Write with Siri, Live Translation, Safari tools, Genmoji and Workout Buddy.[1] Siri AI is described as available in beta in iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27 and visionOS 27.[1]

Languages and regions

Apple Intelligence is listed as available in English, Danish, Dutch, French, German, Italian, Norwegian, Portuguese, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish, Vietnamese, Chinese (simplified), Chinese (traditional), Japanese and Korean. The device language and the Siri language must be set to the same supported language.[3]

  • Siri AI is available "in English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish to start".[1]
  • Siri AI "will not be initially available in the EU on iOS, iPadOS, and watchOS", and is not available to users under 13.[1]
  • Apple says many features are available in most regions, and that Apple Intelligence features "will not currently work" on supported devices bought in mainland China.[3]
  • A full list of countries: not stated.

Storage, limits and cost

  • Storage: up to 14 GB on the newest iPhones, on iPad with M4 and later and on Macs with M3 and later (12 GB or more memory); up to 8 GB on other models; up to 1.5 GB on Apple Watch.[3]
  • Features that rely on server-side models are subject to daily usage limits. Apple says expanded access "will be available for a fee in the future". The fee: not stated.[3]
