Where it lives

Both features are inside the VoiceDebrief iOS app, in the public repository github.com/VoiceDebrief/VoiceDebrief__App__iOS (code under Apache 2.0, content under CC BY 4.0). It runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 26, is written in SwiftUI and uses the Apple SDK only. It uses Apple's on-device models only: SpeechAnalyzer for transcripts and the Foundation Models framework for text. There is no API key and no backend of ours.[7][8]

Owner mode (R6)

A section for one person, the site's owner. It shows every agent from the biggest to the smallest, with its status, what it is doing, what it waits for and its home repository. It also shows the latest websites, videos, briefs and decision slides, and the decisions waiting for the owner. The owner answers there, or uses "talk to this page", typed or spoken, to reach the agent that made it. Decisions and the owner's own actions are listed apart.

  • It reads one JSON contract, owner.feed/v1, from three sources: a demo feed with invented data, published public feeds, and the owner's own encrypted vault.
  • Answers are sealed to an agent's lane. Live data and sending are limited to devices the owner registers with their own key. The app checks the owner's grant against a pinned owner fingerprint, which is not yet pinned.

Sites, the Sovereign Website app (R7)

This is tier 1 of the app. You scan a Sovereign Website's QR code, which carries a key the site's keeper issued and which goes to the Keychain. You see the site in a web view, pick what your comment is about, speak (transcribed on the device) or type, add up to four photos and send. The request is signed and sealed as the site's own send page does it and added to the keeper's lane. The keeper, an agent, answers; the app opens the reply on the device and says when the site has changed.

Tiers 2 (edits on the device with Apple Intelligence, through tools) and 3 (a site from photos and a theme) are proposed, not built.

A demo of the Sovereign Website app on iOS is planned for Thursday 26 November 2026.

Build and TestFlight state

  • The work is on the branch work/sites and is green in continuous integration (build plus unit tests on iPhone and iPad simulators) at commit ee83b1a.
  • None of it has run on a real device yet.
  • It is not on the main development branch and not in TestFlight. A green push there uploads a TestFlight build, and that waits for the owner's yes.
  • The App Store: not stated.
